  - step: 1
    term: 'Reactive (Run-to-Failure) Maintenance'
    slug: 'reactive-run-to-failure-maintenance'
    definition: 'Fixing things after they break. Sensible for cheap, non-critical items, costly when used for critical ones.'
  - step: 2
    term: 'Preventive Maintenance'
    slug: 'preventive-maintenance'
    definition: 'Doing maintenance on a fixed schedule or usage interval to reduce the chance of failure.'
  - step: 3
    term: 'Condition-based Maintenance (CBM)'
    slug: 'condition-based-maintenance-cbm'
    definition: 'Doing maintenance when measurements show it is needed, instead of on a fixed calendar.'
  - step: 4
    term: 'P-F Curve'
    slug: 'p-f-curve'
    definition: 'A model showing that most failures give warning signs: point P is where a potential failure can first be detected and point F is where it actually fails. The gap is your window to act.'
  - step: 5
    term: 'Predictive Maintenance'
    slug: 'predictive-maintenance'
    definition: 'Using data and models to forecast failures so work happens just before it is needed.'
  - step: 6
    term: 'Criticality Analysis'
    slug: 'criticality-analysis'
    definition: 'Ranking assets by how much their failure would affect safety, environment, service and cost, so effort goes where it matters.'
  - step: 7
    term: 'FMEA / FMECA'
    slug: 'fmea-fmeca'
    definition: 'Failure Modes and Effects (and Criticality) Analysis: listing each way an asset can fail, what happens when it does, and how serious it is.'
  - step: 8
    term: 'Reliability-Centered Maintenance (RCM)'
    slug: 'reliability-centered-maintenance-rcm'
    definition: 'A structured method for deciding the right maintenance for each asset based on its functions, how it can fail and the consequences of failure.'
  - step: 9
    term: 'Failure Code / Failure Class'
    slug: 'failure-code-failure-class'
    definition: 'A structured hierarchy used to record why something failed. It runs from failure class to problem to cause to remedy.'
  - step: 10
    term: 'MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures)'
    slug: 'mtbf-mean-time-between-failures'
    definition: 'The average time an asset runs between failures. Higher is better.'
  - step: 11
    term: 'MTTR (Mean Time to Repair)'
    slug: 'mttr-mean-time-to-repair'
    definition: 'The average time it takes to repair an asset after a failure. Lower is better.'
  - step: 12
    term: 'Asset Performance Management (APM)'
    slug: 'asset-performance-management-apm'
    definition: 'Using live data, health scoring and prediction to keep assets reliable and spend money where it matters most. In MAS this is mainly Monitor, Health and Predict working with Manage.'
  - step: 13
    term: 'Maximo Monitor'
    slug: 'maximo-monitor'
    definition: 'The MAS application for collecting and watching live data from sensors and systems, detecting anomalies and showing it on dashboards.'
  - step: 14
    term: 'Maximo Health'
    slug: 'maximo-health'
    definition: 'The MAS application that gives each asset a health score and helps decide what to repair, replace or watch based on condition, risk and cost.'
  - step: 15
    term: 'Health Score'
    slug: 'health-score'
    definition: 'A number, usually 0 to 100, showing an asset''s overall condition, calculated from contributors you choose.'
  - step: 16
    term: 'Maximo Predict'
    slug: 'maximo-predict'
    definition: 'The MAS application that uses machine learning models to predict things like failure probability, predicted failure dates and anomalies.'
  - step: 17
    term: 'Maximo Visual Inspection (MVI)'
    slug: 'maximo-visual-inspection-mvi'
    definition: 'The MAS application for training computer vision models to spot defects, corrosion, leaks or other conditions in images and video.'
